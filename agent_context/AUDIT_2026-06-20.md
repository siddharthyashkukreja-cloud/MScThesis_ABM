# Critical second-reader audit — 2026-06-20

Full audit of code, design, docs, calibration, data, and the regulatory/historical claims, run by six
parallel specialist passes (market, clearing, calibration/validation, fundamental/data, documentation,
regulatory-web). Code is treated as ground truth; the scariest findings were re-verified by hand (noted
**[verified]**). Severity: **P0** = wrong result / indefensible claim / data integrity; **P1** =
important methodology/robustness/reporting; **P2** = polish. Context: a widened-bounds surrogate re-lock
is mid-flight, so several calibration numbers in the docs are knowingly stale — those are consolidated
into one item, not repeated.

---

## VERDICT

The **clearing layer is genuinely strong** and the **regulatory framing is mostly accurate**. The
**market layer and the data pipeline are where the real problems are**: a corrupt stressed data file, a
calibration anchored to a fundamental that is the realised price itself, and a momentum mechanism that is
largely inert. None is fatal, but three data-integrity P0s and two market-mechanism P0s should be fixed
or explicitly disclosed before submission, plus a handful of cheap factual/citation corrections.

## P0 — fix or disclose before submission

### Data integrity (highest priority — re-verified by hand)

**P0-D1 — the stressed processed data file is corrupt. [verified]**
`data/processed/ES_front_stressed_1m.csv`: `volume == 0` for **100%** of 29,077 bars (sum 0) and
`mid == close` for **100%** of bars (calm is healthy: volume 63.6M, mid≠close). Consequences: (i)
`data/impact.py` filters `volume>0` → **0 rows for stressed**, so the stressed impact/η numbers cannot be
reproduced (any re-run → NaN); (ii) the central justification of `v_kalman`/`v_gbm` — "calibrate on the
BBO mid to avoid bid–ask bounce" — is **false for the entire stressed regime** (mid is a copy of the
bounce-prone trade close); (iii) the stressed column of the data-summary table and the franke MCR targets
are computed on close-only data. Fix: rebuild `ES_front_stressed_1m.csv` from the real stressed OHLCV+BBO
feed (the calm path in `data/roll.py` works; check the `.dbn.zst` merge in `data.py` didn't fall back to
close), then re-run stressed impact + v_gbm. Minimum: disclose the limitation prominently.

**P0-D2 — fundamental/impact params calibrated on different (older, larger) windows than the current data. [verified]**
`output/v_gbm_params.json` was fit on calm **n_days=257** (full 2019) and stressed **n_days=29**, but the
current `data/processed/` files are calm **75** sessions and stressed **87**. `output/impact_params.json`
v0's (calm 2463.5, stressed 3252.25) likewise belong to the old windows. So the SV-MJD table in
`calibration.tex` mixes a full-year σ_d with a 75-session sim and **is not reproducible** by re-running
`data/v_gbm.py calibrate` on the committed data (re-calibration moves σ_d by −21%/−33%, λ 0.97→1.37,
etc.). Note also `globals.SIGMA_V` (used by the IM engine and Almgren-Chriss) is a *third* window again.
Fix: regenerate `v_gbm_params.json`, `impact_params.json`, and `SIGMA_V` on the locked windows, re-transcribe
the table, and state one window per regime everywhere (data-summary table already uses the current window,
so the two Chapter-4 tables currently describe two different datasets).

**P0-D3 — the "Kalman-smoothed fundamental" applies no smoothing; V_t is the contemporaneous realised mid. [verified]**
`data/v_kalman.py` `generate()` writes the observed mid as `V_smooth` (calm `max|V_smooth−mid| = 3e-12`).
There is therefore **no two-sided-smoother look-ahead** — good — but the honest framing is different from
what the docs say: the agents are handed the *realised efficient mid at t* and asked to add microstructure
around it (t-measurable, defensible), which is *why* the low-frequency stylised facts are imported from
data rather than generated (see C-P0/identifiability below). Fix: rewrite the `v_kalman` docstring and the
`calibration.tex` fundamental paragraph — defend on **t-measurability**, not "the smoother removes noise";
do not claim a smoother is applied. (Also the docstring's σ_ε figures are wrong for stressed, ~50× off —
P0-D1 contaminates it.) NB the stressed `fv_stressed.csv` differs from the corrupt processed mid by up to
204 pts, i.e. it came from a *different* (older, good) stressed file than the one now in `processed/` — a
provenance mismatch to resolve alongside P0-D1.

### Market mechanism

**P0-M1 — the momentum channel is largely inert, so volatility clustering is not agent-generated.**
`model/agents.py:294-295` updates `M_t=(1−λ)M_{t-1}+λ·r` with `mt_lambda=0.05` on 1-min returns; the
stationary `|M_t|` (~6e-6) sits essentially **at the `mt_eps=1e-6` trigger floor**, so the MT trades on
sign noise, not trend. Worse, `agents.py:307-322` makes MT demand a **sign-only** order (`side=sign(M)`,
size `~U[1,10]`) — the defining saturating **`β·tanh(γM)`** demand of the Extended Chiarella model is
**dropped entirely**. Together these mean the "Chiarella momentum → clustering" mechanism the thesis
claims is mostly absent; the clustering that exists is inherited from `V_t`. Fix: scale MT activation/size
by `tanh(γ|M_t|)` and/or scale `mt_eps` to the regime return scale; report the fraction of steps MT
actually trades. At minimum, disclose the sign-only simplification and re-attribute clustering.

**P0-M2 — the call auction clears every cross at the resting ask, manufacturing bid-ask bounce.**
`model/lob.py:167` sets `clear_px = best_ask` for all crossed pairs (a real call auction uses a single
uniform clearing price), and `lob.py:213-217` falls back to `mid = last_price` when a side empties. With
replace-on-new agents flipping net pressure each step, the print alternates ask/bid → **mechanical
negative lag-1 return autocorrelation** — which is itself a calibration target (ACF1≈0). Risk: the
calibrator is partly fitting a microstructure artefact, not agent dynamics. Fix: clear at a uniform price
(midpoint of the crossing interval or volume-maximising price), carry the previous mid (not last fill)
when a side empties, then re-check ACF(1) survives.

**P0-M3 — calibration runs on an uncapped market; the results run with position caps on.**
The behavioural θ is calibrated with `n_bcm=0` (no clearing tier, so `_client_cap_qty` is a no-op), but at
runtime `run_simulation.py:167` gives every FT/MT/ZI a `clearing_member_id`, so the `cash/(im%·…)` position
cap **binds** (`agents.py:99`) — exactly under stress. The code asserts "θ carries straight over"
(`run_simulation.py:48`) without checking. Fix: either calibrate with caps active (same wiring as runtime),
or measure and report the cap bind-frequency at the calibrated cash levels to justify transfer.

### Calibration / validation

**P0-C1 — `calibrate.py`'s J-test is wrong (use `franke_diagnostics.py`).**
`scripts/calibrate.py:794-799` (`moment_coverage_ratio`) computes J on the **M-averaged** moment vector
then `p = mean(j_null ≥ j_stat)` — averaging ~50 runs shrinks the model spread ~M-fold so J≈0, p≈1
**regardless of fit**. `scripts/franke_diagnostics.py:115-118` is correct per Franke eq. 9 (bootstrap-J
95th-pct critical value; p = fraction of model runs below it). Fix: delete/replace the `calibrate.py` J
block; report only the `franke_diagnostics` p-value. (Low impact in practice since the overnight pipeline
uses `franke_diagnostics`, but the wrong one must not be quoted.)

**P0-C2 — the ACF lag set is the author's own, not XGB-Chiarella's; the text claims faithfulness.**
Code uses `ACF1_CENTERS=ACF2_CENTERS=(1,5,10,20)` with forward-3-lag smoothing and `|r|` for ACF2.
XGB-Chiarella uses ACF1 lags **{1,10,20}** and ACF2 over **all lags 1..20 on squared returns**. The added
5-min centre and the `{1,5,10,20}` set are reasonable but are *deviations*; `calibration.tex` presents the
lag list as "following Gao 2022." Fix: state plainly "we add a 5-min centre to the paper's {1,10,20} and
apply the same set to ACF2 on |r|." (The |r|-vs-r² choice is already correctly flagged.)

### Clearing

**P0-CL1 — cover-2 default fund is sized on the NET book while IM is charged GROSS.**
`clearing.py:179` sizes SLOIM on `abs(book_position)` = |own + Σclient| (net), but IM uses gross client
notional (`CLIENT_MARGIN_NETTING="gross"`). For an NBCM with offsetting client books the netted exposure
≈0 while gross exposure/IM are large, so cover-2 is **understated** and the `(stress−im_ref)·E` coefficient
is applied to the wrong base. Fix: size the default fund on the same gross exposure used for IM, or
explicitly justify net-DF-vs-gross-IM as a modelling choice.

## P1 — important

- **IM "RiskMetrics λ=0.94" is a category error.** `globals.py:333` runs an 11-*day* half-life EWMA on
  1-min bars (per-bar λ≈0.99984); λ=0.94 is a *daily-return* convention. Re-cite as "11-trading-day
  half-life applied to intraday bars," drop the λ=0.94 implication.
- **Waterfall L4 assessment is weighted by survivor *cash*, not DF contribution** (`clearing.py:248`).
  EMIR assesses pro-rata to default-fund contributions. Either weight by `df_contribution` (consistent
  with L3) or document the "ability-to-pay" variant — it changes the contagion topology you study.
- **Identifiability must be foregrounded, not buried.** Only `zi_alpha` is sharply identified; calm D is
  flat over `ft_sigma_c∈[0.5,0.95]` and the three methods disagree on it. This is honestly disclosed
  (`calibration.tex:264-271`), but lead the validation with it and frame the flat levers as "set to
  reasonable values, not identified," with 1-D loss slices.
- **"A value of D near one is a one-σ miss per moment" contradicts the locked D≈18–44.** Drop the
  "near one" sentence or recast the scale; report per-component Δ_c (already computed) as the primary
  readout. (`calibration.tex:142`.)
- **Inverse-SD "scale-pathological" justification is shaky.** Reframe 1/s as keeping components
  numerically commensurate (all in SD units, L1-summed); 1/s² *over*-concentrates on the most precisely
  measured moment. Drop "pathological." (`calibration.tex:139-141`.)
- **Impact β is inflated by a contemporaneous signed-volume proxy** (`impact.py:152`; corr(sign(r)·V, r)
  =0.73 by construction). Use a lagged/tick sign or the permanent-impact regression; treat η as an upper
  bound (it feeds the AC fire-sale).
- **ZI placement is mislabelled "Cont-Stoikov."** It is `Geometric(p)` (exponential decay), not the CST
  power-law `λ(i)∝α/i`. It's a fine data-fit, but relabel ("data-fit geometric, in the spirit of CST/
  Farmer") and stop attributing the geometric to CST. Same for the flat ZI cancellation vs CST's `θ(i)·x`.
- **MPOR=2d, Reg 1.17, SITG, cover-2 — precision fixes (web-verified):** Reg 1.17 = **8% customer + 4%
  non-customer** risk margin (not flat 8%); SITG cite is **EMIR Art. 45(4) + RTS 152/2013 Art. 35** (not
  "EMIR Art. 35"); cover-2 = "largest, or 2nd+3rd if larger" (EMIR 42(3)); 2-day MPOR is the EMIR net-
  omnibus convention (US/CFTC is 1-day gross; EMIR 3 cut some accounts to 1-day in 2024).
- **eSLR: state it as a *finalised* rule, not proposed (web-verified).** Final rule 25-Nov-2025, effective
  1-Apr-2026; holdco = 3% + ½·G-SIB surcharge (~3.5–4.25%, uncapped); IDI ~3–4% (1% buffer cap). Your
  instinct was right; just cite the dates and split holdco vs IDI.
- **Use a current ES margin (~$23k IM, ~6% of ~$380k notional); avoid the stale ~$5,060 figure.**
- **OU (Stein-Stein) vol can go negative and is floored at 1e-10** (`v_gbm.py:155`), attenuating
  vol-of-vol; the EIV vol-of-vol estimator silently falls back to noisy moments when `g2<g1`. Prefer
  OU-on-log-σ / CIR; report the floor-hit fraction. (Robustness arm only.)
- **40:20:40 population ratio is asserted, not sourced;** given M-P1, show the market isn't degenerate to
  FT+ZI. The per-agent FT `z~N(0,1)` heterogeneity is well-motivated — keep it.
